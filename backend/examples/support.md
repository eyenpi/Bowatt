# Northstar support policy

This is a fictional document for testing retrieval, not a policy of this application.

## Enterprise

Enterprise customers receive an initial response within four business hours.
Critical incidents are escalated after one hour without a response.

Requests should include:

- The affected service
- The incident start time
- A short description of the impact

## Standard

Standard customers receive an initial response within two business days.
Email is the only supported contact channel for this plan.

## Ticket examples

The example below contains a comment, not a document heading.

# Include the incident reference
